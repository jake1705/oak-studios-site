In development

Spell Quest

A spelling adventure for ages 4–7. Kids pick an animal buddy, spell to feed and grow it, and explore phonics lands as they learn. No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts — just play.

Approach

Small scope. Close attention.

We keep projects focused, so every detail gets the time it needs.

  • Clarity first

    We define what the software should do, and for whom, before writing code.

  • Platform conventions

    We follow the patterns people already know, so apps feel familiar from the first tap.

  • Safe for kids

    Our children’s apps have no ads, no in-app purchases, and no accounts. We collect nothing from kids — play stays on the device.
