Common questions

What's free, and what does Premium include?

The painting feed, search, favorites, the Daily Puzzle, puzzles up to 40 pieces and the first course are free. Scroll Art Premium adds every course, Gallery Mode, Expert and Master puzzles (70 and 100 pieces) and saving paintings to Photos.

How much is Premium?

Premium is $4.99 a month, or $29.99 a year with a 7-day free trial. Prices can differ by country; the Premium screen in the app shows the price in your currency.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Open Settings in the app, tap Premium is on, then Manage subscription. You can also go to the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid being charged for the next one. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I subscribed but Premium is locked. How do I restore it?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

How do I show paintings on my TV?

Open Gallery Mode from your favorites, choose a set, and start it. Then open Control Center and choose Screen Mirroring to show it on an Apple TV or AirPlay-compatible TV. Turn your phone sideways to fill a widescreen TV.

Can I hide nudity?

Yes. In Settings, turn on Hide nudity. Works with nude figures, including classical ones, are left out of the feed, search, puzzles and Gallery Mode.

Does progress carry over to a new device?

Favorites, finished lessons and puzzles are saved on the device, not online, so they don't sync between devices. They are kept in your device's own iCloud or computer backup, so restoring a backup brings them back. Premium always restores on any device signed in to the same Apple ID.

Does it work offline?

Scroll Art needs an internet connection to load paintings. Your favorites, progress and settings are stored on the device.

Where do the paintings come from?

Scroll Art only shows public-domain works, with images from museums' open-access collections and Wikimedia Commons.

Does Scroll Art collect any data?

No. See the privacy policy.

Reporting a problem

Email support@oakstudiosllc.com and include, if you can:

  • your device (for example, iPhone 15 or iPad Air) and iOS version
  • which screen, painting or lesson the problem happened on
  • what happened, and a screenshot if it helps
