Common questions

Who is Spell Quest for?

Children in kindergarten through 2nd grade (about ages 5 to 8). It follows the order phonics is usually taught at school, from short vowels to tricky spellings.

What's free, and what does the purchase include?

Cozy Woods, the first land, is free. One purchase unlocks all seven lands, every lesson and up to three players, each with their own heroes and progress. It's a one-time purchase: no subscriptions and no ads, ever.

Can my family share the purchase?

Yes. The full quest works with Family Sharing, so everyone in your Apple family group can use it on their own devices.

How do I open the grown-ups' settings?

Tap the gear button on the title screen or the map, then answer the grown-up question. Settings, the weekly summary and the purchase are all there.

I bought the full quest but it's locked. How do I restore it?

Open the grown-ups' settings and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID used to buy it.

Can my child start in a later land?

Yes. In the grown-ups' settings, choose Where to start. Each land shows example words, so you can pick the one that fits. Earlier lands stay open to play for stars.

Does progress carry over to a new device?

Progress is saved on the device, not online, so it doesn't sync between devices. It is kept in your device's own iCloud or computer backup, so restoring a backup brings it back. The purchase always restores on any device.

How do I start over?

With the full quest, use another of the three players for a fresh start. Deleting the app erases all progress on that device; if you reinstall, restore the purchase from the grown-ups' settings.

Can I limit play time?

Yes. In the grown-ups' settings, turn on Break reminder. After the time you choose, your child's hero gets sleepy and suggests a break at the next natural stopping point.

Can I turn off music or the spoken words?

Yes. The grown-ups' settings have separate switches for music and effects, and for speaking each word.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Spell Quest works fully offline. It only goes online through Apple to make or restore a purchase.

Does Spell Quest collect any data?

No. See the privacy policy.

Reporting a problem

Email support@oakstudiosllc.com and include, if you can:

  • your device (for example, iPhone 15 or iPad Air) and iOS version
  • which land and level, or which screen, the problem happened on
  • what happened, and a screenshot if it helps
